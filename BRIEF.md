# Project brief: SchoolSidekick
## The competition
- I’m entering the Presidential SI Challenge (Standard Track), a national K–12 competition. My age group: Middle school (grades 6–8)
- Judges score only a written entry (a PDF of up to 10 pages) and a video of up to 4 minutes. They reward a clear understanding of the problem and a sensible use of AI.
- My deadlines: Deadline for registrations and deadline for submitting entry. Ask teacher to register you before December 18, 2026, 5:00 pm Eastern and submit your writing and video before December 23, 2026, 5:00 pm Eastern (2:00 pm Pacific).
## The problem
- People in school struggle with school work and feeling comfortable in classrooms because they don't understand or don't learn the same or as fast as other people and they are not comfortable with teachers and other students. Teachers in school struggle with grading work, making lesson plans, and creating test because they don't have time outside of school and it will be very stressful.
- Impact area: School and learning
- Why it matters (impact): This will improve peoples lives because they will do better in school and will understand more about things in the grade and will be less stressed outside of school
- Evidence it’s real: Homework can be a major source of stress. In a study described by Stanford University in 2014, 56% of the students surveyed said homework was a main source of stress. The study included 4,317 students at 10 high-performing high schools in wealthier California communities. It shows a problem among those students; it does not represent every U.S. student.
Website: Stanford University—Stanford Report.
Exact page: Stanford research shows pitfalls of homework

Person C: "In English, when I have to take notes, my mind just wander off and then I don't have any notes which means I have nothing to study for or look back too when testing or doing homework."

## My solution
- My AI’s job: My AI helps by answering student questions and helping then with work, which works better than going into a test not being prepared in any way because it can let students improve and do better in school. My AI also helps by giving test and answers to them and making a whole lesson plan for teachers to teach during classes.
- Tools that already exist, and how mine is different: Khanmigo by Khan Academy and MagicSchool. Mine is different because it is free and it doesn't just help with one problem like homework but it can help students with multiple academic problems at school and even for teachers.
- Safety plan: Physical safety: Dangerous advice. Someone could ask about unsafe experiments, medicine, or hurting someone.
Plan: Refuse dangerous instructions, suggest safe activities, and direct students to a teacher or trusted adult.

Emotional safety: Bullying or unkind answers. The AI could embarrass students or help someone insult classmates.
Plan: Require kind, patient language. Refuse bullying requests and include a “Report this answer” button.

Emotional safety: Serious distress. A student might say they feel unsafe or want to hurt themselves.
Plan: Respond calmly and encourage immediate help from a trusted adult. If there is immediate danger, direct them to local emergency help. Explain that the AI is not an emergency service.

Wrong answers or unfair grades. Students could study incorrect information, and teachers could receive incorrect answer keys or grading suggestions.
Plan: Use teacher-approved sources and show explanations. Teachers must check AI-created tests, lesson plans, and grades before using them.

Off-topic questions or cheating. Students could ask unrelated questions or request answers to a real test.
Plan: Redirect unrelated questions to schoolwork. Give hints and practice questions. Keep real tests and teacher answer keys in a teacher-only area.

Data security: Private information getting shared. Users might enter names, grades, passwords, or personal worries. Other students, staff, or the AI company might see saved information.
Plan: Collect as little information as possible, protect accounts, explain who can read chats, and delete information when it is no longer needed. Tell students never to enter passwords.
## My MVP features (build these first, in this order)
1. The user can ask the AI a school question and get a step-by-step hint.
2. The user can get practice questions on a topic to prepare for a test.
3. The user can, as a teacher, ask the AI to draft a lesson plan.
4. The AI gives correct, safe answers to real questions.
## Added features
